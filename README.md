💗 ABOUT THE PROJECT

What if your mirror could become your stage?

BLACKPINK Mirror is an experimental web experience inspired by the world of BLACKPINK, K-pop culture and futuristic digital aesthetics.

Designed as an interactive virtual dance studio, it brings together creativity, technology and the excitement of performing your favourite dance covers.

This isn't just another fan website.

It's your moment in the spotlight.
